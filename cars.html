Car No1: 1981 Fiat Bertone Ritmo S85 - 1.5L 86HP

This Car is my current main Project. It is a lovely piece of Italian Design. Originally I wanted a Golf 2 or a Kadett C - but while I was looking for those, this car kept getting cheaper and cheaper. I had to get it. It has a lot of the same parts as a Fiat 128 or even some Ladas. It was supposed to be a "Golf Killer"... ...Doesnt seem like that worked out very well as noone knows what this car is! But now you do! A cool little Fun fact: In all my research, the Super 85 Version of the Ritmo made 81,82,83,84 and 86 Horsepower! Never 85!
